# CV Product and UX Tailoring Proposal

## Aim

Make product engineering, practical UX judgment, user-workflow design, and design collaboration more visible for relevant applications. Keep the CV evidence-led; do not present the candidate as a dedicated visual or UX designer.

## Candidate updates

- **HSBC (2005–2007):** Add the Flash-based Training & Development portal redesign. The 2006 portfolio case study says it addressed speed and content discovery, added video and an alphabetized quick-access index, and included planning, layout design, implementation, testing, and rollout. It also reports delivery in three months and load time reduced to less than one quarter of its previous duration. Treat these figures as self-reported historical claims unless independently substantiated.
- **HSBC certification tool:** Consider a bullet on the test-authoring interface designed for staff without database knowledge, supporting randomized assessments across seven job programmes.
- **UNBOX (2009–2017):** Emphasize digital learning products across e-learning, game-based learning, LMS, and websites, plus collaboration with clients and multidisciplinary design and development teams. The user reports sustained design/UX focus and close work with designers; existing records do not establish a five-year span or personal UX ownership across all projects.
- **UNBOX naming:** Earlier resumes use “UNBOX Design Studio”; later sources use “UNBOX Learning Experience.” The user reports that the company began as “UNBOX DS” and later changed its name. Confirm the name sequence before representing both in the CV.
- **Earlier GPAC experience:** Consider a compact earlier-career line for agency-based web and learning-content development. A 2006 CV supports web, e-learning, e-books, e-presentations, and multimedia; the roughly six-year total and collaboration with copywriters/graphic designers are user-reported and not detailed in reviewed records.
- **Gamesys designer collaboration:** Make explicit the documented work with designers and stakeholders to resolve design and implementation issues.
- **CDP Status Checker:** Candidate end-to-end product example based on the user’s report of designing the UX/UI and Figma files and implementing the tool. Existing records confirm the tool’s existence and use, but not this ownership scope; corroborate with project/design artifacts before finalizing.
- **Alfred:** Keep as the strongest current example of product and UX/UI work from scratch: mobile flows, onboarding, recommendations, planning, interface, architecture, implementation, and testing. Do not imply user validation, adoption, or market outcomes, which are not documented.
- **Retain workflow outcomes:** Preserve Signal’s self-service template-management experience and Gamesys’ promotional authoring, preview, and delivery tools as product-engineering examples that show how workflows changed for administrators and content teams.

## Evidence and review limits

The HSBC case study is a 2006 portfolio account and supports specific project detail, but it is not independent performance measurement. UNBOX name history and sustained UX emphasis, GPAC collaboration details, and CDP ownership are based partly or wholly on the user’s later recollection. Treat these as candidate claims pending confirmation or artifacts. Keep collaborative ownership and distinguish design judgment and implementation from professional visual-design expertise.

## Next step

Review the canonical experience section against these candidates, confirm the UNBOX naming history and CDP evidence, then select only the highest-value bullets for a UX/product-oriented CV variant. This proposal does not authorize edits to canonical CV content.
